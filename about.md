I'm Jane, a cybersecurity professional with a passion for understanding how things work and how to keep them secure.

I hold the **CISSP** certification from ISC2 and I'm currently studying for the **CCSP** (Certified Cloud Security Professional). My interests span cloud security, governance, risk and compliance (GRC), and hands-on technical security.

When I'm not working, you'll find me tinkering with my **Raspberry Pi home lab**, exploring new security tools, and figuring out how to break (and fix) things in a safe environment.

I started this blog to document my learning journey, share what I pick up along the way, and hopefully help others who are navigating a similar path in cybersecurity.

### What I write about

- Certification journeys (CISSP, CCSP, and beyond)
- Home lab projects and experiments
- Cloud security concepts
- Career reflections and lessons learned
