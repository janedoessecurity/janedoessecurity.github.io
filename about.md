**JaneDoesSecurity**
Documenting my journey through cybersecurity — one lab, book, and certification at a time.

Hi, I'm Jane — a cybersecurity professional with a passion for continuous learning.

I currently hold the **CISSP** certification and I'm currently studying for the **CCSP**. My interests span several areas of cybersecurity, including security architecture, incident response, governance, risk and compliance (GRC), and hands-on technical security.

Outside of work, I enjoy exploring cybersecurity in different ways. This includes reading security-themed books (which inspired the [Security Bookshelf](/bookshelf/) section of this site), working through hands-on [labs](/labs/) to build practical skills, listening to the Darknet Diaries podcast while doing housework, and writing about what I learn.

I started this blog as a place to document my learning journey, share ideas, and reflect on the topics that interest me in cybersecurity.

### What I Write About

On this blog you'll find posts about:

- **Certification journeys** – studying for certifications such as CCSP and others in the future
- **Hands-on labs and learning notes** – practical security exercises and lessons learned
- **Security books** – reflections and insights from books I've read
- **Career reflections** – lessons learned while working in cybersecurity
